## Writing

The editor now shows you what you just wrote. Type, and the new words carry a wash of your accent colour that fades on its own in about a second. There is nothing to clear and nothing to turn off — it dries.

It earns its place on a long note. After a paste, a toolbar insert, or a mention that completes itself, the caret has already moved on and the text you just added looks exactly like text you wrote an hour ago. The wash is the difference, and it is gone before it can become clutter.

The caret is a block now instead of a hairline, so where the next character lands is visible at a glance — including the moment it jumps to a new line.

Both follow your accent colour, so they change when your theme does.
